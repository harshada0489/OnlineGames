🌿 About

Daily Games is five small puzzles, one new set every day. The same puzzles for everyone in the world on the same date.

No login. No accounts. No ads. No trackers. Your progress lives only in your browser. Clear your browser data and it's gone — nothing is saved on any server.

Each game takes a minute or two. When you finish today's puzzle, that's it — come back tomorrow. The idea is calm, not addiction.

How it works

Every puzzle is generated from a small seed: today's date, minus the launch date. That's why everyone sees the same colour, maze, sequence, and pattern on the same day. There is no server. The whole site is just static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

What we don't do

No analytics. No fingerprinting. No third-party scripts. No newsletter pop-ups. The only thing that talks to the network is the page itself loading.
